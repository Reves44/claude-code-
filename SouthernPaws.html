About Us

Southern Hospitality for Your Four-Legged Family

At Southern Paws, we bring old-fashioned care and attention to every groom. Located right here in Oliver Springs, we know your pets by name — and we treat them with the love they deserve.

Stress-Free Experience

We take our time with every pet, ensuring a calm, comfortable grooming session they'll actually enjoy.

Safe & Professional

All tools sanitized between every appointment. Your pet's safety is our top priority, always.

Always On Time

We respect your schedule. Book online and get a text confirmation — no waiting, no surprises.

What We Offer

Full-Service Grooming

From a quick tidy-up to a head-to-tail spa day — we have a service for every pup.

Full Groom

Complete bath, blow-dry, haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning — the full Southern Paws experience.

Bath & Brush

Deep cleansing shampoo, conditioning rinse, professional blow-dry, and thorough brush-out.

Puppy's First Groom

A gentle introduction to grooming for puppies. We make their first visit fun and stress-free.

Nail Trim & Grind

Precision nail trimming and grinding for smooth, safe paws. Quick add-on or standalone service.

De-Shedding Treatment

Specialized treatment to dramatically reduce shedding. Perfect for heavy-coat breeds year-round.

Ear Cleaning & Plucking

Gentle ear cleaning to prevent infections. Plucking available for breeds that require it.
