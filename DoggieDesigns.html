Our Story

Craft. Care.
Character.

Doggie Designs on Clinton Hwy has been Powell's go-to grooming destination for discerning pup parents. Under Erica's leadership, the team has built a reputation for flawless cuts, genuine warmth, and results that speak for themselves.

We treat every dog as an individual — reading their temperament, working at their pace, and tailoring every groom to bring out their best. Soft, fresh, and smelling amazing every single time.
