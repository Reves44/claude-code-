Our Story

A Spa Experience Worthy of Your Best Friend.

Dog Days Inn Grooming & Spa was born from a simple belief: every dog deserves to feel pampered, safe, and loved — not just clean. Nestled on Clinch Ave in Clinton, TN, we've been the trusted choice for pet parents who want more than a basic grooming appointment.

Our groomers treat every dog as an individual. We take the time to understand your pup's temperament, coat needs, and personality — so they leave looking their absolute best and feeling completely at ease.
