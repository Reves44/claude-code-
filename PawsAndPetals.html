About the Studio

Crafted for dogs
who deserve the best.

At Paws & Petals, grooming isn't a transaction — it's a relationship. Abby takes the time to get to know your dog, understand their personality, and create an experience that's calm, comfortable, and truly loving.

The studio is kept clean and serene — no chaos, no assembly line. Each appointment is given undivided attention, so your pup gets the care they actually deserve.
