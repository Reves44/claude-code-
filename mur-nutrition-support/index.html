FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Once your order ships you'll receive a tracking email. Orders typically ship within 1–2 business days. If you haven't received a tracking number after 3 business days, contact us below and we'll look into it right away.
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unopened products. If you received a damaged or incorrect item, we'll make it right at no cost to you. Contact us with your order number and a photo of the issue to get started.
Log in to your account and go to "Subscriptions" to pause, modify, or cancel at any time. Changes made before your next billing date will take effect immediately. If you need help, send us a message below.
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password via email. If you're still having trouble, contact our support team with the email address associated with your account and we'll get you back in.
Full ingredient lists and supplement facts panels are listed on each product page. If you have a specific allergy concern or need additional information, reach out to us and we'll provide detailed information.
Enter your promo code in the "Discount Code" field at checkout before completing your purchase. Codes are case-sensitive and can only be used once per account unless stated otherwise. Expired or invalid codes cannot be applied retroactively.
Currently we ship within the United States. We're working on expanding international shipping. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when your country becomes available.
Double charges are usually a temporary authorization hold that clears within 3–5 business days. If the charge is still showing after that window, contact us with your order number and we'll investigate immediately.

Support team is available. We typically respond within 1 business day.
