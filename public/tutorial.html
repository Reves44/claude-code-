Getting Started

Create your account and set up your profile

1

Download the App

Search "MUR Nutrition" in the App Store (iOS) or Google Play and install the app on your device.

2

Create an Account

Open the app and tap Sign Up. Enter your email address and create a password. You can also sign in with Apple or Google for a faster setup.

3

Enter Your Body Stats

Input your age, height, weight, and biological sex. This data powers MUR's AI recommendations and calorie/macro targets.

4

Set Your Goals

Choose your primary goal — lose weight, build muscle, improve endurance, or eat healthier. You can update this anytime.

5

Connect Apple Health (Optional)

Open iPhone Settings → Health → Sources and tap MUR Nutrition to allow syncing. This lets the app read your steps, workouts, and vitals automatically.

6

Explore Your Dashboard

You'll land on your personal dashboard showing daily nutrition targets, upcoming workouts, and AI coach suggestions.

💡 Be accurate with your body stats — MUR's AI uses them to calculate your personalized calorie and macro targets. You can always update them later in Account → Settings.
2

Food Scanner

Instantly log any meal with your camera

1

Open the Scanner

Tap the camera icon or the Scan button on the home screen or bottom nav bar.

2

Point at Your Food

Hold your phone over the food item or meal. Make sure it's well lit and the food fills most of the frame.

3

Tap the Scan Button

Tap the scan button and the AI (powered by OpenAI/Google Vision) will identify the food within seconds.

4

Review Nutritional Info

MUR shows you calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, and more. Adjust the portion size if needed.

5

Log the Meal

Tap Add to Log to save it to your daily nutrition diary. It instantly updates your macros and calorie totals.

✅ The scanner works best with whole food items (fruit, vegetables, meat) and common packaged foods. For complex mixed dishes, you can also search by name manually.
🔒 Food photos are sent to AI services (OpenAI/Google) only to identify food content — they are not stored or shared for any other purpose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers straight from the MUR Nutrition support page

How do I reset my password? ▾
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link shortly. Check your spam/junk folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▾
You can cancel at any time through your device's App Store settings:
iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → MUR Nutrition → Cancel.
Or contact the support team at support@murnutrition.com.
How does the food scanner work? ▾
Point your camera at any food item or meal and tap the scan button. MUR's AI (using OpenAI/Google Vision technology) will identify the food and provide detailed nutritional information including calories, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber within seconds.
Can I connect Apple Health? ▾
Yes! Open the Health app on your iPhone, go to Browse → Sources, and tap MUR Nutrition to manage sync permissions. You can control exactly what data is shared.
How do I delete my account? ▾
Go to Account → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete Account". This will permanently erase all your data including food logs, workout history, and personal stats. This action cannot be undone.
What data does the app collect? ▾
MUR collects your account info, body stats, nutrition logs, workout history, and goals to power personalized recommendations. Your food photos are analyzed by AI services (OpenAI/Google) solely to identify nutritional content and are not stored beyond that process. Read the full Privacy Policy for complete details.
Is there a free version? ▾
MUR Nutrition offers a free tier with core features. Premium subscription unlocks full AI coaching, unlimited food scanning, advanced analytics, and access to the supplement shop. Subscription can be managed through your App Store settings.
