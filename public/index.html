Our Story

Passionate About Pets Since Day One

At Pampered Paws Grooming, we believe every dog deserves to look and feel their absolute best. Located at 7544 Oak Ridge Hwy #2 in Knoxville, we've been serving the community with professional, compassionate grooming for over a decade.

Our certified groomers treat every pet like their own — with patience, expertise, and a whole lot of love. We use only premium, pet-safe products that leave your fur baby smelling amazing.

  • Certified professional groomers with 10+ years experience
  • Premium, pet-safe shampoos and conditioners only
  • Stress-free environment for anxious pets
  • Instant SMS notification sent to our phone on booking
  • Breed-specific grooming techniques for every pup
Our Services

Everything Your Pup Needs

From a quick refresh to a full luxury spa day — we have the perfect service for every dog.

Bath & Brush

Full shampoo and conditioning treatment with premium blow-dry and brush-out for a fluffy, fresh finish.

Full Groom

Complete breed-specific haircut, bath, blow-dry, nail trim, ear cleaning and bandana — the whole package.

Puppy's First Groom

A gentle, fun introduction to grooming designed specifically for puppies under 6 months old.

Luxury Spa Package

Blueberry facial, oatmeal soak, teeth brushing, paw balm, and aromatherapy rinse — pure indulgence.

Nail Trim & File

Quick, safe nail trimming and smooth filing to keep your pup comfortable and your floors scratch-free.

De-Shed Treatment

Specialized deshedding shampoo, conditioner, and blow-out to dramatically reduce shedding at home.
